## 👋 About Me

I am **Kennedy Maurice**, a Kenyan healthcare professional, entrepreneur, and software developer building technology-driven businesses and digital platforms.

My background combines **Medical Laboratory Science, healthcare operations, medical-device distribution, institutional management, procurement, software engineering, infrastructure, and product development**.

That combination shapes the kind of systems I enjoy building: not software in isolation, but technology designed around **real operational problems, business processes, healthcare workflows, users, data, and scale**.

I hold a **Bachelor of Science in Medical Laboratory Science** from the **Technical University of Kenya** and have expanded my work into software engineering, digital product development, healthcare entrepreneurship, AI-enabled workflows, and institutional development.

> **Healthcare gives me the domain context. Engineering gives me the tools. Entrepreneurship makes me think about the entire system.**

# 🧭 Product Philosophy

I prefer building technology around a few principles:

**1. Solve the workflow, not merely the screen.**
A beautiful interface is useful only when the underlying process makes sense.

**2. Architecture should reflect the business domain.**
Good schemas and APIs begin with understanding how the organisation actually operates.

**3. Design for evolution.**
Production systems change. Architecture should accommodate growth without making every new requirement a rewrite.

**4. UX and engineering are not separate disciplines.**
Performance, information hierarchy, accessibility and workflow design are all part of product quality.

**5. Technology should reduce operational friction.**
The best system makes complicated work feel simpler.
